# Business Block

Full business website — company info, services, team, contact form, FAQ, and testimonials. Adapts to any trade via vibe content.

**Triggers**: business, website, landing, services, locksmith, dentist, plumber, electrician, mechanic, roofer, cleaning, contractor, hvac, painter, photographer, studio, salon, spa, fitness, gym, lawyer, notary, consultant, agency

**Env vars**:
| Variable | Default | Description |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| `BUSINESS_NAME` | — | Business name |
| `BUSINESS_ADDRESS` | — | Street address |
| `BUSINESS_PHONE` | — | Phone number |
| `BUSINESS_EMAIL` | — | Contact email |
| `BUSINESS_HOURS` | — | Operating hours |
| `SERVICE_AREA` | — | Geographic service area |

**API endpoints**: `/api/business/info`, `/api/business/services`, `/api/business/team`, `/api/business/contact`, `/api/business/faq`

**Dependencies**: (none)
