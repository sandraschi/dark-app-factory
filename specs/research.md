# Domain Research: Austrian Dentistry SOTA 2026

## 1. Legal Requirements (Impressum & GDPR/DSGVO)
- **Impressumpflicht**: Mandatory for commercial sites in Austria. Must contain:
  - Full name/legal structure.
  - Non-P.O. box address (**Schlossgasse 1, Hollabrunn**).
  - Email & Phone (contact form alone insufficient).
  - Professional title & country of origin.
  - Affiliation: **Landeszahnärztekammer Niederösterreich**.
  - Reference to legal basis (Zahnärztegesetz).
- **DSGVO**: Dental data is "special category" health data.
  - Explicit consent for health records.
  - Data breach notification within 72 hours.
  - Right to data portability and deletion.
  - Avoid "Dark Patterns" in consent manipulation.

## 2. Features & UX (Non-Intimidating Dentistry)
- **Top Competitor Features**:
  - Calm, neutral palette (Sage Green, Sand, Off-White).
  - "Pain-Free" USP (Unique Selling Proposition) emphasis.
  - Easy booking: No-login requests, status heartbeat.
- **Micro-Animations**: Subtle entrance animations for trust-building.

## 3. Technical Standards
- **Database**: Must support pseudonymisation and 10-year data retention (legal requirement for medical records in Austria).
- **Security**: AES-256 for health descriptions, TLS 1.3 for communications.
