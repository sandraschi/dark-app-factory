# The Vibe: Joe Painlessdriller's Dentist App 🦷

> "I want a dentist website for my friend Joe Painlessdriller in Hollabrunn, Schlossgasse 1. It must meet strict Austrian standards but be totally non-intimidating."

## Customer Requirements
1.  **Public Website**:
    -   Address: **Hollabrunn, Schlossgasse 1**.
    -   Detailed, reassuring service menu (Checkup, Hygiene, Implants, "Pain-Free" promise).
    -   Appointment Booking System (easy, no login required for request).
    -   **Aesthetics**: Beautiful, clean, modern, calming. "Copying of beautiful competition sites is allowed."
2.  **Dentist Portal (Admin)**:
    -   Secure Login for Joe and staff.
    -   Patient Database: Personal Info, Treatment History, Treatment Plans.
    -   Appointment Management.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend**: node/express
- **Frontend**: react
- **Database**: postgresql

## Technical Constraints
-   **MUST WORK**: No placeholders, no empty pages.
